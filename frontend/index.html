About the system

Feedback that actually reaches the right people

Paper forms get lost and are hard to analyse. CampusVoice collects feedback online, stores it in a secure cloud database, and gives administrators live analytics to act on it.

Simple form

Pick a category, rate with stars, describe your experience and suggest improvements.

Stored in the cloud

Responses are saved in a managed PostgreSQL database — available from anywhere, never lost.

Live analytics

Admins see ratings by category, trends over time, and can export reports in one click.

Private & secure

Your details are visible only to authorised administrators. Passwords are hashed and sessions protected.

How it works

Three steps from your phone to the principal's dashboard

Optionally create a student account to keep a private history of everything you've submitted.

Submit Feedback Create student account View my submissions
1
Fill the feedback form

Choose a category, give a 1–5 star rating and describe your experience. The form checks your input before sending.

2
Securely sent to the cloud

Your browser sends the data over HTTPS to our Flask API, which validates it again and saves it to Supabase PostgreSQL.

3
Reviewed by administrators

Admins log in to a protected dashboard to search, filter, analyse and export feedback — and act on it.
